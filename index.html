SCHOOLS • UNIVERSITIES • INSTITUTIONS

TURN YOUR
STUDENTS INTO
OPPORTUNITIES.

Jodex Football Academy works with schools and universities to build structured football programmes that develop students technically, physically, mentally and professionally.

From grassroots development and after-school training to competitive teams, talent identification and progression pathways, we help institutions give their students a stronger sporting future.

01

ON-CAMPUS TRAINING

Professional coaching sessions designed around your timetable, facilities and student age groups.

02

TALENT DEVELOPMENT

Identify promising players early and give them a clear development plan toward higher-level football.

03

COMPETITION & PATHWAYS

Prepare school teams for competitions while creating pathways into academies, universities and elite opportunities.

04

COACHING & FOOTBALL EDUCATION

Support your institution with football programmes, coaching structures, player assessment and development guidance.

FOR SCHOOL & UNIVERSITY ADMINISTRATORS
